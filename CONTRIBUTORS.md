# Contributors

Everyone who has contributed to andeye, and – by adding their name here in
their first pull request – recorded their agreement to the Contributor
Licence Agreement ([CLA.md](CLA.md)).

Adding your name is how you sign: it means "I have read and agree to the
CLA" for this and all future contributions. A pull request from an author
not listed here (or not stating their agreement in the PR itself) can't be
merged.
