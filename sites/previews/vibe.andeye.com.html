Vibe andeye

vibe coding without the slop

Letting an AI write your code shouldn’t mean accepting whatever it produces. Vibe andeye puts adversarial agentic models between your idea and your codebase: one plans, one builds, and independent critics, testers and reviewers attack the result until it actually holds up.

How it keeps the quality up

  • A secure sandbox. Agents work in an isolated container with a firewalled network and per-action permission gates - they can build your project, not wander your machine.
  • Adversarial by design. The model that writes the code never marks its own homework. Spec critics, testers and reviewers are separate agents with an incentive to find fault.
  • Tests before trust. Work is gated on real, runnable acceptance criteria wherever they exist - and on independent expert review where they don't.
  • For developers first. And increasingly for non-coding experts who know exactly what they need - the harness supplies the engineering discipline.
