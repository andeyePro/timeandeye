andeye colour lab

A playground for the colour-strategy proposals in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-06-colour-strategy.md. Everything below runs the actual candidate algorithms, live, in this page – nothing is a mockup screenshot.

1. Stability

The recommended allocator (hue neighbourhood, strategy C below) colouring a live project/task tree. Click "Add project" or "Add task" repeatedly – the newcomer's swatch flashes so your eye can track it, and every OTHER swatch on the page stays byte-identical. "Check stability" re-derives every existing swatch's colour from its stored assignment and reports how many drifted (should always read zero).

2. Swatch editing

Click ANY swatch below to open the native colour picker and override it. This is the same live tree as panel 1 – flip back after editing and the override is still there. A small dot marks a swatch you've overridden.

Overrides survive. There is deliberately no "re-spread all" button here or anywhere on this page – the stability principle means colours only ever change because you picked one.

3. Strategies side by side

The same 4 projects × 5 tasks, allocated by three different rules, in the same arrival order. Fixture names were chosen so a real hash near-collision shows up under strategy A.

4. Colour-blind view

These four buttons re-render every swatch on the ENTIRE page (all four panels, not just this one) through a colour-vision-deficiency simulation. Flip to another tab while a simulation is active to see it applied there too.
