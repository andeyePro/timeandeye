Something wrong, or just not sure how something works? Here's where to go.

## Everyone: bugs and ideas on GitHub

Time&I is developed in the open at
[github.com/andeyePro/timeandeye](https://github.com/andeyePro/timeandeye). Found
a bug, or have an idea for a feature? That's the place, and it's free for
everyone, no andeyePro subscription needed.

You don't need to be a developer to use it. Here's the walkthrough:

1. **Make a free GitHub account**, if you don't already have one - go to
   [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup) and follow the prompts.
2. **Go to the timeandeye repository**:
   [github.com/andeyePro/timeandeye](https://github.com/andeyePro/timeandeye).
3. **Pick Issues or Discussions**, near the top of the page.
   - Something is broken, or behaving in a way it shouldn't - open an
     **Issue**. Click **New issue** and describe what happened.
   - You have a question, or an idea for something andeye could do - open a
     **Discussion** instead. It's a friendlier space for "what if" and "how
     do I".
4. **Write it up.** For a bug, the more detail the better - but even a rough
   description is worth posting. If you can, include:
   - Your macOS version (Apple menu ▸ About This Mac).
   - Your Time&I version (open Settings from the Time&I menu - it's shown at
     the top).
   - What you expected to happen.
   - What actually happened instead.
5. **Click Submit.** Someone will read it - often quickly, sometimes it takes
   a few days if it's a quiet week.

That's genuinely it. You're not bothering anyone; this is what the GitHub
page is there for.

## andeyePro: a fast reply by email

If you're an andeyePro subscriber, you don't need to go via GitHub at all -
email **[time@andeye.com](mailto:time@andeye.com)** and you'll get a reply
quickly. This is the supported channel that comes with a Pro subscription:
a real person, answering your specific question about your setup.

## Why the difference exists

andeye's tracker is free forever, and so is the community help on GitHub -
reading an issue, answering a question, fixing a bug, all of that costs
nothing to ask for. Sitting down and answering *you*, personally, by email,
takes real time on someone's part, and that's one of the things an andeyePro
subscription pays for. So if you'd like hand-held support (or you want Xero,
or the iPhone companion app), andeyePro is how you fund the free tool as
well as get the extras.
