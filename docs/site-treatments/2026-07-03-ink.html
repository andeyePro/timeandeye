01method

How it works.

1

Observe

andeye notices which window and tab are frontmost – on your Mac, using your Mac. That stream of attention is all it collects, and it stays where it was made.

2

Attribute

Each minute is matched to a task from your own backlog, and the match carries an honest certainty figure. 96% means it knows. 44% means it asks you rather than guessing quietly.

3

Post

Time you confirm – or that clears your certainty threshold – posts to your own backlog. OpenProject today; more to come.

02the point

Nothing leaves your Mac.

Automatic time tracking is only tolerable when nobody else can read the record. So andeye keeps the whole apparatus local: observation happens on your machine, attribution happens on your machine, and the learning does too.

Your day is a file on your disk, not a row in somebody's database. When you teach it a rule – mail from this client → this project – the rule is written to your disk and consulted there. There is no account to create, no telemetry to opt out of, no server waiting for a sync that never needs to happen.

This is the reason the product exists, not a feature added later.
