How it works

01Observe

andeye reads the title of your active window and tab, on-device, as you work. That signal is input, not upload – it never leaves the machine.

02Attribute

Each minute is matched to a task in your backlog and scored. 72% certain means 72% – the number is the matcher's honest output, never rounded up to reassure you.

0%certainty100%

03Post

Time you confirm is booked to your own backlog. OpenProject today; more connectors to come.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

Observation, attribution and learning all run locally. There is no server of ours to trust, because there is no server of ours. The one thing that ever leaves is a time entry you confirmed, posted to a backlog you own.

Window and tab titleswhat andeye sees on-device · never leaves
Attribution and certainty scoringhow it decides on-device · never leaves
Pinned ruleswhat it learns from you your disk · never leaves
Confirmed time entrieswhat you choose to post your backlog · the one place it goes

When you pin a rule – mail from this client → this project – it is stored on your disk, applied by your Mac, and yours to read, change or delete. Your working patterns are not a product. They are your property.
